Rules & contact

Picboard is a shared wall. Keep it fun for everyone.

The rules

  1. Nothing illegal. If it's against the law to share, it doesn't go here.
  2. Zero tolerance for anything sexualizing minors. It's removed, the poster is banned, and it's reported to the authorities.
  3. No porn, gore, or shock content.
  4. No harassment, hate, or threats toward anyone, on the board or off it.
  5. No personal info. Don't post anyone's address, phone number, or private details, including your own.
  6. No spam or scams.
  7. Only post what you have the right to share.

How moderation works

Anyone can report a pin, note, or drawing by right-clicking it (or tapping the ⋯ button). Anything reported by three people is hidden for everyone until a moderator reviews it, and child-safety reports hide an item immediately. Images are also automatically checked against known illegal material. Moderators can remove content and ban people who break the rules.

Privacy

You don't need an account. Your browser gets an anonymous ID so the site knows which things you posted. Photos are resized and have location data stripped before they're uploaded. Videos are uploaded as-is, so check them yourself. Other people in a room can see your cursor and the name you choose. If you save an account, your email is only used to send you sign-in links.

Contact & takedowns

Email for anything else, including copyright takedown requests. For copyright, include a link to the room, which item it is, and how you own it.
